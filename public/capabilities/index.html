7,617 files. 100% success on the tiers that ran. Sixteen worker containers at 4 GB. PostgreSQL tuned — shared buffers 4 GB, max WAL 8 GB — on fastpool, a 16K-recordsize SSD mirror. The ceiling is architectural

Ten- and fifty-megabyte files process cleanly at high throughput. A 100 MB or 200 MB file is base64-encoded into one message — about 140 MB and 279 MB — and parsed entirely in worker memory, on the order of 3–6 GB for 250–500 thousand claims. That overwhelms the broker and the per-worker budget no matter how many workers you add. Next unlock: chunk the file at the interchange boundary before enqueue, for files past roughly 50–70 MB.

  1. 01

    Government-grade X12 validation

    Integrity through implementation-guide checks, a loop-aware parser, and machine acknowledgments. Every issue is anchored to its segment, element, and loop.

  2. 02

    Coverage and coding scrub

    NCCI, NCD/LCD, modifier relationships, sequencing, eligibility, and duplicates — driven by versioned rule tables, not rules welded into the code.

  3. 03

    FHIR R4, both directions

    CARIN and Da Vinci profiles between legacy transactions and modern resources. The bridge, not a rewrite of the last thirty years.

  4. 04

    Tiered AI, supervised

    A fast path for conforming work. Harder cases go to scored tiers and specialist agents under a supervisor policy. Confidence and trigger reasons are recorded for replay.

  5. 05

    Tamper-evident provenance

    Deterministic record identity and an append-only Merkle journal. When “trust us” is not good enough, the math is.
